I am a Graduate Researcher at the University of Melbourne. My research focuses on interpreting human behavior and social interactions from audiovisual observations, with interests in theory of mind (ToM), nonverbal behavior modeling, and social dynamics understanding and prediction.

Previously, I received my Master's degree from Yonsei University under the supervision of Prof. Youngjung Uh, where I focused on dynamic 3D scene reconstruction using 3DGS and NeRF. I also collaborated with Taein Kwon on dynamic 3D hand reconstruction.

Research

In the long term, I hope to develop socially intelligent embodied agents, such as humanoid robots, that can consider both physical and social contexts and collaborate with people in everyday life.
